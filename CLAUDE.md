# panicci-content-engine: rules for Claude Code

Client and Panicci content (shoots, presets, hyperframes video). The per-project notes live in
`video/hyperframes/*/CLAUDE.md`. This rule applies to all of it.

## Founder photo law (non-negotiable)
**No cut-off shoulders. Ever.** (Sammy's rule, 2026-11-08, permanent.) Anywhere Sammy's photo
appears (site, story frames, carousels, cards, ads, thumbnails, video), both shoulders show whole,
with their natural rounded edge. The bottom edge is the only crop allowed: the torso can run off the
bottom of the frame or under a footer band. A shoulder or sleeve never touches or runs off the left
or right edge of the frame, a box, a circle or a mask, and never ends in a straight vertical cut.
- Use a cutout whose shoulders are whole: `panicci-os/assets/headshots/sammy-tee.png` (880x789, its
  sleeves touch the canvas sides only in the bottom 12%) or, on the site, `sammy-skeptic.webp`
  (640x592, bottom 6%). Hide at least that bottom slice under the band or the frame's bottom edge.
- `sammy-smile.webp` (sliced in the bottom 31%), `sammy-smirk.webp` (bottom 34%) and the daily
  engine's `sammy-charcoal.png` (right side, bottom 40%) are sliced flat by their own canvas. Use
  them only where that whole slice is hidden; otherwise use a whole-shoulder cutout.
- Keep at least 24px between the outer shoulder and the side of the frame.
- Never repair a sliced shoulder by generating, outpainting, stretching or mirroring. His likeness
  stays his real photo.
- Before shipping, render it and look at both shoulders.
