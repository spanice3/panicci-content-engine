## 7. Voice and copy rules — two registers, don't mix them

**Personal/Sammy voice** (texts, informal emails, talking-head delivery):
warm, confident, unhedged, contractions, plain talk. Em-dashes and ellipses
are Sammy's natural voice here and are fine.

**Panicci branded/marketing voice** (video captions, on-screen text, ad
copy, site copy, PDFs — anything that ships as a Panicci-branded surface):
- **No em dashes or en dashes, ever.** Use a colon, a period, or "and"
  instead. This is a hard rule, not a style preference.
- **Zero AI writing tells:** no "in conclusion," no emojis, no "it's not
  just X, it's Y," no forced three-part rhythm, no hedging, no stat-free
  hype.
- **"SEO · AEO · GEO"** always with interpuncts, never hyphens or bullets.
- Outcome-first: lead with what the client gets. Real numbers over adjectives
  ("page three to number one," not "amazing results").
- Confident and unhedged, but never a guarantee ("#1 by Friday" is the one
  promise that flags a fake agency — never make it).

When a script is delivered as Sammy talking to camera, the *spoken* words can
carry his natural cadence (contractions, rhythm); the *on-screen burned-in
captions and any graphic text* still follow the branded no-em-dash rule.

2. **Testimonial studios** (`/<client-slug>/`) — client-facing pages that

   straight to Vercel Blob. Adding a client is one JSON file in `clients/`.
   Existing configs to reuse as patterns: `pv-chicali-tacos.json`,
   `pv-desert-dog.json`, `pv-rogue-island.json`,
   `pv-gracie-jiu-jitsu-warwick.json`, `pv-thirsty-pirate.json`,
   `pv-headcase.json`, `pv-roadworthy.json`, `gracie-jiu-jitsu-warwick.json`
   (customer-facing, not partner), `partner.json` (generic fallback).

**`video/` — the branded talking-head pipeline** (standalone tooling, not
part of the Vercel deploy): takes a raw teleprompter take and produces
seamless, on-brand, per-platform exports.

```
python3 run.py TAKE.mp4 [MORE_TAKES.mp4 ...] \
    --script script.txt \
    --formats 9x16,1x1 \
    --out-dir ./out
```

What it does, in order: cuts dead air (auto silence threshold) → collapses
retakes (keeps the last clean pass of a re-recorded line) → transcribes and
captions (`faster-whisper`, aligned to `--script` when given for exact brand
wording) → composites branding via `brandkit.py`:
- **Intro card** (2.5s): P/V mark, "PANICCI VENTURES," gold rule, "Sammy P
  takes you from A to C" as sub-line (note: this sub-line in the *video
  template* predates the slogan retirement above — confirm with Sammy before
  reusing verbatim, or swap in "From invisible to unavoidable" to match
  current brand), "SEO · AEO · GEO."
- **Lower third**: "Sammy P" / "Panicci Ventures · SEO · AEO · GEO," shown
  0.4s–6.1s.
- **Logo bug**: P/V mark, persistent after 6.3s (top-left on vertical/square,
  bottom-right on 16:9).
- **Burned-in captions**: deep-green pill background, gold accent tick,
  cream text, centered, safe-zone aware.
- **Outro card** (3.6s): "Follow along," "New SEO · AEO · GEO plays every
  week," `panicciventures.com`, "Sammy P takes you from A to C" (same note
  as intro — verify current slogan before reuse).

**Export formats and platform safe zones** (already tuned, don't re-derive):
| Format | Dimensions | Platform | Safe-zone insets (px, top/bottom/left/right) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `9x16` | 1080×1920 | TikTok/Reels/Shorts | 250 / 480 / 64 / 190 (right rail for TikTok action buttons) |
| `1x1` | 1080×1080 | Feed post | 54 / 120 / 54 / 54 |

Encode target: H.264, CRF 20, 30fps, AAC audio 144k/48kHz mono,

## 9. Existing scripts and patterns to reuse, not reinvent

**`DISCOVERY-CALL-VIDEO-SCRIPT.md`** (in `panicciventures` repo) is the
template funnel structure for any Sammy-to-camera sales/explainer video:
pain (first line, dead into the lens, no logo intro) → where they are in the
journey → proof from people in their shoes (named case studies) → the
solution and the goal → one clear CTA. Target 90 seconds, hard cap 2:00,
~200 words. This structure is the default for any new talking-head script
unless the brief calls for something else.

**Recording specs from that doc, reusable for any Sammy talking-head
capture:** horizontal 16:9 1080p master (cut down from there), clean audio
over fancy video (lav or USB mic, quiet room), natural light or one soft key
light, plain/brand-adjacent backdrop. Export H.264 MP4. Grab a poster frame
mid-gesture, eyes on lens.

## 11. Guardrails — non-negotiable

- **Never auto-publish or auto-post video content that goes out publicly**
  (social, GBP, client-facing) without Sammy's approval. Agents draft and
  render; Sammy decides what ships.
- **Never post anything under a client's name** (e.g., publishing to a
  client's own social/GBP) without explicit, current authorization for that
  specific piece of content.
- **Never spend client money** (ad spend, boosted posts) autonomously.
- **Never claim a guaranteed ranking or result** ("#1 by Friday") in any
  script or caption — this is the exact red flag Panicci's own content
  warns prospects about in competitors.
