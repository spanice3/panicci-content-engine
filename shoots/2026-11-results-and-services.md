# Shoot day: results + services (Nov 2026)

Everything is loaded in the studio. Two places, same facts:

- **📜 Free script → 📼 Ready scripts.** My Story plus ten straight talking heads. Each take is cut as one captioned video, and its `.json` carries the IG caption, so posting is copy and paste. These go to the grid.
- **🎯 50·5·3 → 📼 Ready 50·5·3 sets.** Four short sets: written bodies, then hooks, then CTAs, one take per set. The Clipper cuts every hook onto the bodies and CTAs, and the **Reel Lab** tests them as trial reels (non-followers first). Only winners reach your followers, when you share them in the Instagram app.

Short on time? Do the four 50·5·3 sets first. They feed the Reel Lab, and the winners end up on the grid anyway.

## How to read them

- **Ready scripts:** read straight through. The script box is spoken words only (the Clipper aligns captions to it), so delivery notes show above the box, not in it.
- **50·5·3 sets:** the badge says **Body → Meat**: read it your way, but keep every number exactly as written. Then hooks (read, one beat, N) and CTAs (read, N). Flub? Say it again before N. No restarts.
- Black tee, window light in front, camera at eye level, **E** for eyeline mode. Talk to one owner, not "you guys."
