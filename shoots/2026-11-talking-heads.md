## Setup (matches the card headshots, so the feed looks like one person)

- **Black tee**, same one as the studio faces. The card build recolors the tee by pixel, so black keeps every new
  photo compatible.
- **Plain light wall** behind you, a few feet back. Clean background = clean cutouts.
- **Window light in front of you**, not behind. Lamp off if it's yellow.
- **Camera at eye level**, phone vertical. Look just under the lens (eyeline mode in the studio helps).
- **Mic**: lav or the AirPods mic beats the laptop mic every time.
- Quiet house window. Girls' nap or school hours.

## Run order

1. **Headshots first** (15 min, fresh face). Shot list below.
2. **The typo test** as the warm-up. Low stakes, gets the voice going.
3. **My Story**: 3 takes. Read it out loud once before the first. Keep the best; the Clipper handles the rest.
4. The other five, 2 takes each. Studio note under the picker tells you the one delivery cue per script.
5. After each keeper: **📤 Send to Clipper**. All of them land in one Drive folder for the day.

Flub a line? Pause, take a breath, say the whole sentence again. The cut keeps the last clean pass.

## The 6 new headshots (real photos, no AI)

Your rule from Sep 22 stands: every face on a card is a real photo of you, never generated or altered. So these get
shot, not made. Each one fills a gap in the current five (smile, laugh, eyebrow, smirk, skeptic):

| Look | How to hold it | Fills this gap |
|---|---|---|
| **point** | Point up and across at where the headline sits. Shoot both sides. | Hook and note cards that need to push the eye to the words |
| **phone** | Phone in hand at chest height, eyes on the screen, "huh" look | The 10-minute "check your profile" cards, now the most common format |
| **concern** | Brows slightly together, small sympathetic wince, mouth closed | Bad-news cards: the rules ban eyebrow and skeptic there, so today they fall back to smile |
| **think** | Hand on chin or two fingers at the jaw, eyes up and off camera | Fill-in-the-blank and "which one are you?" cards |
| **crossed** | Arms crossed, chin level, confident half-smile at the lens | Stat and proof cards (client wins, real numbers) |
| **explain** | Both hands open mid-gesture, mouth mid-word | Teardown and story cards (how I look at it) |

Specs so they drop straight into the card build next to the existing five:
- Same framing as the current faces: top of the head near the top third, **full shoulders in frame**, chest up.
  The card bleeds your shoulders off the bottom edge, so don't crop them.
- Rear camera, 1x, Portrait mode **off** (it smears hair edges and wrecks the cutout).
- 5–8 frames per look. Send the originals (HEIC is fine) to the shoot's Drive folder or text them over.

Once they're in: clean cutouts, tee recolors, and the new faces added to the card build's face rules so the
daily engine picks them by card type.

| Day | Post |

Two steps stay manual because Instagram's API can't do them: **pinning** and **adding to a highlight**. Both take
under a minute in the app. Everything else goes through the OS: reels queue in `social_queue` and wait for your
approval on the Social tab, same as now.

Check after a week: reel reach and profile visits vs the cards' ~35 reach. If the talking heads win (they should),
the next shoot is a 50·5·3 session for ads.
